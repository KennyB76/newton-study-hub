Problem 1 — Plant Asset Sale (Kimera Company)

Kimera Company sold a machine for $8,500 that cost $50,000. Book value at sale = $12,500. Prepare the journal entry.

Trinity 답 검증 ✅ 완벽

Trinity 답 = 100% 정확.
  • Cash $8,500 (받은 현금) ✓
  • Accumulated Depreciation $37,500 (= $50,000 cost − $12,500 book value) ✓
  • Loss on Sale $4,000 (= $12,500 book value − $8,500 cash) ✓
  • Equipment $50,000 (cost 제거) ✓

Final Journal Entry

Cash 8,500 Accumulated Depreciation 37,500 Loss on Sale of Equipment 4,000 Equipment 50,000

핵심 logic (메모)

Step 1: Cash 받은 만큼 ↑
Step 2: 그 자산의 Accumulated Depreciation 전부 제거 (= Cost − Book Value)
Step 3: 그 자산의 Cost 전부 제거 (Equipment Cr)
Step 4: 차액 = Gain (Cash > Book) or Loss (Cash < Book)
→ 이 경우: Cash $8,500 < Book $12,500 → Loss $4,000

Problem 2 — Bond Issue at Discount (West End Corporation)

On January 1, 2024, West End Corporation issued $500,000, 5%, 5-year bonds dated January 1, 2024, at 96. The bonds pay semiannual interest on January 1st and July 1st. Prepare entries for issuance and first interest payment.

Trinity 답 검증 — 정정 필요

Trinity 답 — Issuance: Cash 500,000 / Bonds Payable 500,000
문제: Issued at 96 = bonds were sold at 96% of face value. Cash received = $500,000 × 96% = $480,000 (NOT $500,000). The $20,000 difference is the Discount on Bonds Payable.

정정된 Issuance Entry

Jan 1, 2024 Cash 480,000 Discount on Bonds Payable 20,000 Bonds Payable 500,000 (Issued $500K face at 96 → received $480K, discount $20K)
왜 Discount? (직관)
Bond 의 stated rate (5%) 가 market rate 보다 낮으면 — 투자자들이 그 가격에 안 사. 그래서 회사가 face value 보다 싸게 (96%) 팔아야 함. 차액 = Discount = "할인된 부분".

Trinity 답 — Semiannual Interest (Straight-Line 가정)

Trinity 답: Interest Expense 12,500 / Cash 12,500
문제: Cash Interest 는 $12,500 맞아 ($500K × 5% × 6/12). 하지만 Discount 가 매 period 마다 amortize 되어야 함. Interest Expense ≠ Cash Interest 야.

정정된 Semiannual Interest Entry (Straight-Line)

Cash Interest = Face × Stated Rate × period
= $500,000 × 5% × 6/12 = $12,500

Discount Amortization (Straight-Line) = Total Discount ÷ # of periods
= $20,000 ÷ 10 periods (5 yr × 2 semi-annual) = $2,000 per period

Interest Expense = Cash Interest + Discount Amortization
= $12,500 + $2,000 = $14,500
July 1, 2024 Interest Expense 14,500 Discount on Bonds Payable 2,000 Cash 12,500 (Cash Int $12,500 + Discount Amort $2,000)

Why Discount Amortization?

Bond 가 discount 로 팔린 이유 = stated rate 가 너무 낮음. Investor 가 5% 보다 더 받아야 만족. 그래서 회사는 face value 보다 적게 받고 (= $480K), but 5 year 후 face value ($500K) 갚아야 함. 그 차액 ($20K) = "extra interest" 인 셈. 매 period 마다 조금씩 expense 인식 = Discount Amortization.

결과: Interest Expense (14,500) > Cash Paid (12,500). 차이만큼 Discount account 가 줄어듬 → Carrying Value 가 face value 향해 ↑↑↑ 올라감.

5 Common Mistakes

  1. Bond at 96 = $500,000 cash 받은 게 아님. 96 = 96% of face. Cash = $480,000. Discount = $20,000.
  2. Bonds Payable account 는 항상 face value. Discount/Premium 은 별도 contra account.
  3. Cash Interest ≠ Interest Expense. Cash Int = Face × Stated. Int Exp = Cash Int ± Amortization.
  4. Discount → Int Exp > Cash Int (add discount amort). Premium → Int Exp < Cash Int (subtract premium amort).
  5. Straight-Line: divide total disc/prem by # of periods. EIM: use carrying value × market rate. 문제에서 어느 method 인지 확인.

6 Key Takeaways

  1. Plant asset sale: Cash + reverse Acc Depr + reverse Cost. Difference = Gain/Loss.
  2. Bond price quoted as % of face. 96 = 96% = $480K on $500K face.
  3. Discount on Bonds Payable = contra-liability account. Reduces Bonds Payable on Balance Sheet.
  4. Carrying Value = Bonds Payable (face) − Discount + Premium. Walks toward face value over life.
  5. Discount: CV ↑ over time. Premium: CV ↓ over time. Both end at face on maturity.
  6. Cash Interest is FIXED ($500K × 5% × 6/12 = $12,500). Interest Expense changes (under EIM).
