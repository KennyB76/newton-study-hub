Bond Issuance — Effective Interest Method

Newton Corp · Premium amortization · 5 parts · 2026-05-08

Question

📝 Given
Newton Corp issues 4-year, $100,000 face value bonds on January 1, 2026. Stated rate = 6% (annual) Interest paid annually on December 31 Market rate at issuance = 5% Issue price = $103,545.95
🎯 Required (5 parts)
  1. (a) Journal entry on January 1, 2026 (issue date)
  2. (b) Bond amortization schedule (4 years, effective interest method)
  3. (c) Journal entry on December 31, 2026 (year 1 interest + amortization)
  4. (d) Carrying value of bonds on December 31, 2027 (end of year 2)
  5. (e) Total interest expense over 4 years

Setup — Key Formulas & Numbers

① Cash Interest (constant every year)
Cash Int = Face × Stated Rate = $100,000 × 6% = $6,000
② Interest Expense (effective interest method)
Int Exp = Beginning Carrying Value × Market Rate (5%)
③ Premium Amortization
Prem Amort = Cash Int − Int Exp
④ Ending Carrying Value
End CV = Beginning CV − Prem Amort

Premium total at issue = $103,545.95 − $100,000 = $3,545.95 (will amortize to $0 over 4 years).

(e) Total Interest Expense (4 years)
Sum of Int Exp column. 또는 shortcut: Total Cash Int − Total Premium amortized.
Method 1 — sum schedule

Method 2 — shortcut

= $24,000 − $3,545.95 = $20,454.05

Total Interest Expense (4 years) = $20,454.05

Common Mistakes

⚠ 시험에서 자주 틀리는 것
  • Stated vs Market rate 혼동: Cash Int = stated × face / Int Exp = market × CV. 절대 헷갈리지 마
  • Premium 방향: issue price > face → premium / Int Exp < Cash Int (premium amortizes down)
  • Discount 의 경우 반대: issue price < face → discount / Int Exp > Cash Int (discount amortizes up)
  • Premium credit balance: Bonds Payable 같이 credit. 갚을 때 (amortize) debit
  • CV 갱신 시점: Year N의 end CV = Year N+1 의 beg CV (이전 row 의 마지막 column)
💡 핵심

Effective interest method = CV × market rate = Int Exp. Cash int 는 항상 같음 (stated × face). 차이가 premium/discount amortization. Premium → CV 줄어들고 Int Exp 도 매년 줄어듬. Total Int Exp = Total Cash − Premium (premium 만큼 적게 expense).
