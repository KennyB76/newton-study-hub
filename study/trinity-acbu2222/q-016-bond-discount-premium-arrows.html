Problem 1 — DISCOUNT (West End at 96)

$500,000, 5%, 5-year bonds, Jan 1, 2024, at 96. Semiannual interest Jan 1 + July 1.

Arrow Diagram — Discount

ISSUANCE (Jan 1, 2024) Cash $480,000 ← paid by investors (96% of face) │ ↓ short by $20K Bonds Payable $500,000 ← what we promise to repay (face) Discount on B/P $20,000 ← contra-liability (the "shortage") Carrying Value at issuance = $500,000 - $20,000 = $480,000 ─────────────────────────────────────────────── EACH SEMIANNUAL PERIOD (10 total periods): Cash Interest = $500K × 5% × 6/12 = $12,500 (FIXED, paid in cash) Discount Amort (Straight-Line) = $20,000 / 10 = $2,000 Interest Expense = $12,500 + $2,000 = $14,500 ↑ (more than cash paid, because discount is being "expensed up") Carrying Value walks ↑↑↑ toward $500,000 ─────────────────────────────────────────────── AT MATURITY (Jan 1, 2029): CV = $500,000 (= face) Discount on B/P = $0 (fully amortized) Cash 500,000 Bonds Payable 500,000 ← repay face

Problem 2 — PREMIUM (West End at 106)

$500,000, 5%, 5-year bonds, Jan 1, 2024, at 106. Semiannual interest.
⚠️ Trinity 답 검증 필요: issued at 106 = 106% × $500K = $530K cash (NOT $500K).

Arrow Diagram — Premium

ISSUANCE (Jan 1, 2024) Cash $530,000 ← paid by investors (106% of face) │ ↓ extra $30K Bonds Payable $500,000 ← what we promise to repay (face) Premium on B/P $30,000 ← liability addition ("extra premium received") Carrying Value at issuance = $500,000 + $30,000 = $530,000 ─────────────────────────────────────────────── EACH SEMIANNUAL PERIOD (10 total periods): Cash Interest = $500K × 5% × 6/12 = $12,500 (FIXED) Premium Amort (Straight-Line) = $30,000 / 10 = $3,000 Interest Expense = $12,500 - $3,000 = $9,500 ↓ (LESS than cash paid, because premium is being "credited back") Carrying Value walks ↓↓↓ toward $500,000 ─────────────────────────────────────────────── AT MATURITY (Jan 1, 2029): CV = $500,000 (= face) Premium on B/P = $0 (fully amortized) Cash 500,000 Bonds Payable 500,000 ← repay face

5 Common Mistakes

  1. "At 96" or "at 106" 의미 놓침. 항상 % of face. 96 = $480K, 106 = $530K on $500K face.
  2. Bonds Payable 에 직접 더하거나 빼지 X. Bonds Pay 는 항상 face value. Discount/Premium 은 별도 contra account.
  3. Cash Interest = Interest Expense 로 잘못 등록. Discount 면 Int Exp > Cash. Premium 면 Int Exp < Cash.
  4. Premium amortization 방향 헷갈림. Premium 은 Cr balance → Dr 로 amortize (감소). Int Exp 에서 빼는 거.
  5. Issuance entry 에서 cash 도 face value 로 적기. 항상 actual cash received 로 적어. Issue date 가격에 따라 다름.

6 Key Takeaways

  1. Bond price = % of face. 96 = 96% = discount. 106 = 106% = premium.
  2. Bonds Payable always = face. Discount/Premium = contra account.
  3. Cash Interest is FIXED = face × stated rate × period. Same regardless of price.
  4. Discount: Int Exp > Cash Int (add disc amort). Premium: Int Exp < Cash Int (subtract prem amort).
  5. Carrying Value walks toward face value. Discount ↑ rising. Premium ↓ falling. Both end at face.
  6. Straight-Line: total disc/prem ÷ # periods. EIM: CV × market rate (more accurate but harder).
