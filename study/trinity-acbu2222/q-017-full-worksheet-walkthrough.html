Section 1 — PLANT ASSETS

Problem: Kimera Company sold a machine for $8,500 that cost $50,000. It had a book value of $12,500 at the time of sale. Prepare the entry to record the sale of the machine for Kimera Company.

Step-by-step (Kimera Plant Sale)

What we knowAmount
Cash received$8,500
Original Cost (Equipment)$50,000
Book Value at sale$12,500
Accumulated Depreciation (= Cost − BV)$50,000 − $12,500 = $37,500
Loss (= BV − Cash)$12,500 − $8,500 = $4,000

Journal Entry — ANSWER

Cash 8,500 Accumulated Depreciation 37,500 Loss on Sale of Equipment 4,000 Equipment 50,000
Logic: Cash 받음 ↑ + sold asset 의 Acc Depr 전부 제거 + sold asset 의 Cost 전부 제거. 차액이 Gain (Cash > BV) 또는 Loss (Cash < BV). 이 경우 Cash $8,500 < BV $12,500 → Loss $4,000.

Section 2 — BONDS at 96 (Discount)

Problem: On January 1, 2024, West End Corporation issued $500,000, 5%, 5-year bonds dated January 1, 2024, at 96. The bonds pay semiannual interest on January 1st and July 1st.

Setup

ItemValue
Face Value$500,000
Stated Rate (annual)5%
Semiannual stated rate2.5% (= 5% / 2)
Issue Price (96% × $500,000)$480,000 cash
Discount on Bonds Payable$20,000 ($500K − $480K)
Total periods (5 yr × 2)10
Cash Interest per period (Face × stated/2)$500,000 × 2.5% = $12,500
Discount Amortization (Straight-Line, $20K / 10)$2,000 per period
Interest Expense per period (Cash + Disc Amort)$12,500 + $2,000 = $14,500

January 1, 2024 ~ Bond Issue/Sale — ANSWER

Cash 480,000 Discount on Bonds Payable 20,000 Bonds Payable 500,000

July 1, 2024 ~ Semiannual Interest Payment — ANSWER

Interest Expense 14,500 Discount on Bonds Payable 2,000 Cash 12,500
Why $14,500 not $12,500? Cash paid is $12,500 (= face × stated × period). But the bond was sold cheap ($480K), so each period the company "expenses up" $2,000 of the discount. Total cost of borrowing per period = $14,500.

Section 3 — BONDS at 106 (Premium)

Problem: Same West End Corporation bonds, but issued at 106 instead of 96. Semiannual interest Jan 1 + July 1.

Setup

ItemValue
Face Value$500,000
Issue Price (106% × $500,000)$530,000 cash
Premium on Bonds Payable$30,000 ($530K − $500K)
Total periods10
Cash Interest per period$12,500 (same as discount case)
Premium Amortization (S/L, $30K / 10)$3,000 per period
Interest Expense per period (Cash − Prem Amort)$12,500 − $3,000 = $9,500

January 1, 2024 ~ Bond Issue/Sale — ANSWER

Cash 530,000 Bonds Payable 500,000 Premium on Bonds Payable 30,000

June 30, 2024 ~ Interest Accrual — ANSWER

If the company is closing books on Jun 30 (before paying cash on Jul 1):

Interest Expense 9,500 Premium on Bonds Payable 3,000 Interest Payable 12,500

July 1, 2024 ~ Interest Payment — ANSWER

If accrual was already done on Jun 30 → just pay cash:

Interest Payable 12,500 Cash 12,500

Alternatively — if no accrual is done first and the entire entry is on Jul 1:

Interest Expense 9,500 Premium on Bonds Payable 3,000 Cash 12,500
Why $9,500 not $12,500? Cash paid is still $12,500 (= face × stated × period). But the bond was sold at a premium ($530K), so each period $3,000 of the premium is "credited back" to reduce expense. Net cost = $9,500.

Section 4 — Contractual Rate (CR) vs Market Rate (MR)

ConditionBond Price AtWhy
If CR > MRPREMIUMStated rate is higher than what market demands. Investors will pay extra to buy this bond.
If CR < MRDISCOUNTStated rate is lower than what market demands. Investors won't buy at face — only at a discount.
If CR = MRFACE VALUE (Par)Rates match exactly. No adjustment needed.
The 3 worksheet blanks → ANSWERS:
① CR > MR → premium
② CR < MR → discount
③ CR = MR → face value

5 Common Mistakes

  1. "Bond at 96" = $500K cash 인 줄. NO — 96 is 96% of face = $480K cash. Same for 106 = $530K.
  2. Bonds Payable account 에 직접 더하거나 빼기. Bonds Pay 는 항상 face value. Discount/Premium 은 별도 contra account.
  3. Cash Interest = Interest Expense 로 잘못 등록. Discount: Int Exp > Cash. Premium: Int Exp < Cash.
  4. Plant sale 시 두 entry 따로 안 쓰기. 한 entry 에 (Cash + remove A/D + remove Cost + Gain/Loss) 다 들어감.
  5. Plant sale 시 Cost 만 제거하고 A/D 까먹기. 두 account 다 제거해야 함 (Cost + Acc Depr).

6 Key Takeaways

  1. Plant sale entry = Cash ↑, A/D ↑(remove), Cost (remove). 차액 = Gain/Loss.
  2. Bond price quoted as % of face. 96 = $480K. 106 = $530K. 100 = face value.
  3. Bonds Payable always = face. Discount/Premium = contra account.
  4. Cash Interest is FIXED = face × stated rate × period (semiannual ÷2).
  5. Discount: Int Exp = Cash + Amort. Premium: Int Exp = Cash − Amort.
  6. CV always walks toward face value. CR > MR = Premium (CV ↓). CR < MR = Discount (CV ↑).
