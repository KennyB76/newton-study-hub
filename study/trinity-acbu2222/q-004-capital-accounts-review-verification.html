Conceptual Q&A — verify

1. Difference between Preferred & Common Stock?
Your answer: Voting rights, Dividends in arrears
✅ Correct
Common = voting rights, residual claim. Preferred = no voting (usually), fixed dividend rate, dividend priority. "Cumulative" preferred can have arrears (unpaid dividends accumulate).
2. What's Excess / Additional Paid-In Capital?
Your answer: Monies above par value
✅ Correct
When stock issues for more than par, the excess goes to APIC. Example: $42 issue − $10 par = $32 to APIC per share.
3. Treasury Stock is what type of account?
Your answer: Contra-Equity
✅ Correct
Treasury Stock has a debit balance and is subtracted from total stockholders' equity at the bottom of the balance sheet.
4. Why do companies issue / acquire stock?
Your answer: Finance themselves, increase market value, decrease shares outstanding
✅ Correct
Issue → raise cash. Buyback (treasury) → reduce shares outstanding (boost EPS), support price, fund stock comp, fight takeover.
5. Retained Earnings affected by which two accounts?
Your answer: Net Income & Dividends
✅ Correct
RE rolls forward: Beg RE + Net Income − Dividends = End RE. (Plus prior period adjustments in some cases.)

Common Mistakes

⚠ The 5 traps on this exact problem type
  • Treasury reissue BELOW cost
    Don't dump the whole shortfall to Retained Earnings. ALWAYS use APIC—T.S. balance first, then RE for any remaining. (You got this right in #4 — keep it.)
  • Outstanding shares vs Issued shares
    Outstanding = Issued − Treasury (held back). Dividend per share uses OUTSTANDING.
  • Incomplete journal entries
    Every JE needs a debit AND a credit. "DVDs Payable 950" alone is wrong format. (Fix in #6 above.)
  • No entry on record date
    Many students try to record something. Don't — record date is admin only.
  • "Cash Dividends" vs "Retained Earnings" on declaration
    Either works (book uses Cash Dividends as a temporary account that closes to RE at year-end). Both treated the same in net effect.
💡 Key Takeaway — 30 sec
  • Issue stock: Cash debit, Common Stock at par, APIC for excess.
  • Treasury (cost method): debit at cost, contra-equity.
  • Reissue ABOVE cost: extra → APIC—T.S. (credit).
  • Reissue BELOW cost: shortfall → APIC—T.S. first, then RE (both debit).
  • Outstanding = Issued − Treasury. Use this for per-share dividend.
  • 3 dividend dates: Declaration (debit Cash Dividends, credit Payable) → Record (no entry) → Payment (debit Payable, credit Cash).
